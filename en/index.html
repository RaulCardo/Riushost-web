Frequently asked questions

What owners usually ask us before getting started

Which areas do you cover?

Valencia city and the towns in its metropolitan area and province. If your property is not on the list in the form, write to us anyway.

Do I need a licence to rent my property to tourists?

Yes. In the Valencian Community the property must be registered in the Tourism Register and comply with its town's planning rules. We guide you through the steps and paperwork.

Can I still use my property?

Yes. We block the dates you want to keep for yourself or your family in the calendar. Just let us know in advance.

Who pays for cleaning?

The cleaning fee is usually paid by the guest when booking. We coordinate the cleaning after every check-out.

What happens if a guest causes damage?

We inspect the property after every stay and, if there is damage, we handle the claim with the booking platform. We also recommend home insurance that covers holiday letting.

How do I know how much my property has earned?

We send you a regular summary of bookings, income and expenses, and you can ask us at any time.
